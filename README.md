# SupplyProof — Supplier Delivery Verification

*A B2B tool for growing restaurant groups and retail businesses.*

## Problem

Restaurant groups, multi-branch cafés, and regional retail businesses receive regular deliveries from multiple suppliers. Receiving teams may struggle to identify, document, and follow up on discrepancies between purchase orders, delivered goods, and supplier invoices.

These discrepancies can include missing items, damaged goods, incorrect products, and billing mismatches. The frequency and business impact of these problems still need to be established through customer research.

## Solution

SupplyProof is a proposed delivery-verification and discrepancy-management tool that helps receiving teams document delivery issues, organize supporting evidence, and track supplier resolutions in one place.

Rather than functioning as a general inventory-management system, SupplyProof focuses on the workflow from identifying a delivery discrepancy to recording its resolution.

## Target Market

Small and medium-sized businesses with recurring supplier deliveries, particularly restaurant groups, multi-branch cafés, and regional retail businesses.

The team will validate the target customer segment with potential users and confirm that it aligns with the hackathon's non-micro-enterprise requirement.

## Core Workflow

1. *Verify delivery:* Record the quantities ordered and received.
2. *Document discrepancies:* Flag missing, damaged, or incorrect items and attach relevant photos or notes.
3. *Check evidence readiness:* Identify missing information or supporting evidence before contacting the supplier.
4. *Track resolution:* Record supplier responses, replacement deliveries, credits, refunds, and case status.
5. *Monitor outstanding cases:* Give managers visibility into unresolved discrepancies and their estimated value across suppliers or locations.

## Proposed MVP

- Delivery discrepancy entry form.
- Expected-versus-received quantity comparison.
- Supporting evidence attachments and a completeness checklist.
- Discrepancy status and resolution tracking.
- Dashboard showing open cases, estimated disputed amounts, and recorded recoveries.

The initial prototype will use manual entry and sample data. Complex integrations, automated supplier communications, and enterprise-system connections are outside the initial scope.

## Business Model Hypothesis

A B2B subscription for businesses that regularly receive supplier deliveries.

Pricing and willingness to pay have not yet been validated. The team must determine whether the workflow provides enough value to justify a separate tool alongside existing systems and processes.

## Current Status

SupplyProof is a proposed hackathon concept, not a validated product. Customer needs, existing alternatives, differentiation, and commercial viability remain to be investigated before the team finalizes the scope.
