# SupplyProof — Supplier Delivery Verification

*A B2B tool for growing restaurant groups and retail businesses.*

## Problem

Restaurant groups, multi-branch cafés, and regional retail businesses receive regular deliveries from multiple suppliers. Receiving teams may struggle to identify, document, and follow up on discrepancies between purchase orders, delivered goods, and supplier invoices.

These issues can involve missing items, damaged goods, incorrect products, or billing mismatches. How frequently these problems occur and how businesses currently resolve them remain to be validated.

## Solution

SupplyProof is a proposed delivery-verification and discrepancy-management tool that helps receiving teams record problems, organize supporting evidence, and track supplier resolutions in one place.

## Target Market

Small and medium-sized businesses with recurring supplier deliveries, such as restaurant groups, multi-branch cafés, and regional retail chains.

The target customer segment must be validated with potential users and confirmed as appropriate for the hackathon's non-micro-enterprise requirement.

## Core Workflow

1. *Verify delivery:* Record the quantities ordered and received.
2. *Document discrepancies:* Flag missing, damaged, or incorrect items and attach photos or notes.
3. *Track resolution:* Record supplier responses, replacement deliveries, credits, refunds, and case status.
4. *Monitor outstanding cases:* Give managers visibility into unresolved discrepancies and their estimated value across suppliers or locations.

## Proposed MVP

- Delivery discrepancy entry form.
- Expected-versus-received quantity comparison.
- Supporting evidence attachments.
- Discrepancy status and resolution tracking.
- Dashboard showing open cases and estimated disputed amounts.

The initial prototype will use manual entry and sample data, without complex integrations or automated supplier communications.

## Business Model Hypothesis

A B2B subscription for businesses that receive regular supplier deliveries. Pricing and willingness to pay have not yet been validated.

## Current Status

SupplyProof is a proposed hackathon concept, not a validated product. The team must investigate customer needs, existing alternatives, and the feasibility of a dedicated discrepancy-management workflow before committing to the final scope.
